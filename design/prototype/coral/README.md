# Coral Garden

The coral is now two small colonies of layered, cupped glass. Three rose and peach forms sit behind a lower pair in seafoam. Each cup has a closed underside and rounded, scalloped rim, with color concentrated toward the center and lighter glass at its edges. The grouped bases meet the sand.

Coral Garden uses the existing lighting, reflections, refraction, shadows, and theme colors. When combined with kelp, the kelp moves behind and to the right to avoid passing through the coral. The renderer and companion route planner share that placement.

The existing Coral Garden selection and saved value are retained. Its description and color swatches now match the sculpture. The widget snapshot cache is v7 so the app regenerates older coral stills.
