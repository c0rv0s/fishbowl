# Fishbowl design explorations

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are visual concepts, not implemented app screens.

## 01-sunroom

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished visual redesign concept for an existing iOS app named Fishbowl. This is a playful personal aquarium app: create named tanks, select fish and habitat, tap the water to feed fish, hum to generate a new bowl, and display saved tanks as Home Screen widgets. No social feed, currency, chores, water chemistry dashboard, or invented reward mechanics.
Deliver ONE landscape image, approximately 3:2, with TWO complete, front-facing iPhone app screens at equal large scale, side by side with clean even margins. Thin identical dark titanium phone frames, subtle shadows, visible status bars and home indicators. No perspective tilt, no hands, no desk photography, no cropped devices. The screens should occupy almost all available image height. This is an actual UI design comparison, with comfortably readable realistic mobile typography and 44pt touch targets. No captions or promotional headings outside devices. No eyebrow labels above headlines. No huge unused canvas.
Both screens must share one coherent visual system, fish artwork, palette and controls. Left is the primary aquarium home. Right is a related bowl customization screen, with the same tank visible as a smaller live preview and a bottom customization sheet. The artwork is the central experience and should be richly authored. UI chrome is restrained.
Left screen: top title "Fishbowl" and a small tasteful overflow icon. A truly memorable aquarium visual occupies roughly the central 55% of the screen. Beneath it, tank name "Little lagoon" and short status "Fed and happy". Small instruction "Tap the water to feed". A slim bottom navigation with three labeled destinations "Bowls", "Create", "Widgets"; Bowls selected.
Right screen: top bar "Cancel" at left, title "New bowl", "Save" at right. The top 45% contains a smaller version of the same aquarium. An anchored bottom sheet has title "Make it yours", three understated categories "Fish", "Bowl", "Habitat" with Fish selected, and one row of three spacious illustrated fish choices labeled "Moon Koi", "Royal Betta", "Ember Tetra". The Moon Koi option is selected and matches the left aquarium. A clearly readable full-width secondary action near the bottom says "Hum a bowl" with a custom small microphone glyph. One clean solid accent primary button above home indicator says "Keep this bowl". Avoid excessive pills, cards within cards, generic outline icons, tiny microcopy, nonsensical text, fake charts, and generic startup gradients. Do not create a website inside a phone.

DIRECTION: SUNROOM. An exquisite tiny living object sitting in morning sunlight. Warm ivory #F4F1E7, deep pine #183E35, fresh celadon #A8C7A7, clear water and a vivid coral-orange koi as the focal accent. The main aquarium is a gorgeous substantial clear handblown spherical fishbowl with an open elliptical lip, real waterline, visibly thick refracting edge, water caustics falling onto a pale limestone ground plane. Within: one beautiful orange-and-cream moon koi with long translucent flowing fins, a miniature composition of mossy stones, tiny fine gravel, graceful green water plants. Artwork looks like a refined high quality stylized 3D diorama, believable glass and sunlight, luminous without overexposure. All objects clearly inside the water. The bowl fills width nearly edge to edge. Subtle warm paper texture in the background and soft cast shadows. Type pairs a graceful medium-weight rounded serif for tank name with clean native iOS sans for controls. Deep pine text has clear contrast. Natural light and beautifully composed miniature aquascaping are the signature. Editor sheet is matte warm ivory, compact 20pt corners, thin pine underline for active category. Fish selection thumbnails are pale celadon with a fine dark outline on selected fish. Main button deep pine. Make this feel like an artful object you would happily keep open on your phone, warm and alive, not beige wellness stock design.

DIRECTION: AFTER DARK. Immersive aquarium as a jewel of light in a dark room. Background near-black petrol #071C22, luminous mineral teal #63C2BC, pale pearl #E8F3EB, and orange-white koi. Visually radically different from a floating bowl on a generic background. Here a tall, architectural clear glass tank extends nearly the full width and central height, integrated with the phone screen as a portal. Thin discernible glass edges, a quiet high waterline, dramatic diagonal natural shafts of light from the upper right, real underwater depth with dark mossy stones and layered fine aquatic plants. One extraordinary orange-and-pearl moon koi, large enough to see graceful translucent tail anatomy, swims through the light; a few tiny suspended particles catch it. Rich premium stylized 3D aquarium rendering, cinematic and immersive with crisp silhouettes. Keep deep dark regions clean rather than noisy. Restrained fine modern sans typography, generous tracking only in the main product wordmark, readable mixed-case labels. The title, tank name and status sit in calm untextured dark regions above/below the aquarium. No cards around the home tank. Navigation uses tiny intentional filled aquatic glyphs and pearl text. Active nav has a small sea-glass dot. Editor sheet opaque dark petrol, hairline separators, teal selected state; bright pale seafoam main button with near-black text. Underwater light must feel optically plausible, not neon, holographic, cyberpunk or purple. The result should feel almost like holding a small illuminated aquarium in your palm.

DIRECTION: POCKET REEF. A delightful authored indie toy aquarium, with high-end illustration and tactile character. Butter-yellow #FAF1C7 and warm white UI, saturated cobalt #234FBB type and controls, clear turquoise #8CCED0 water, vermilion-orange #EA6444 koi, mossy green plants. Use an illustrated cut-paper and hand-painted gouache visual system: a beautifully shaped large rounded-rectangle aquarium with thick slightly wobbly cobalt illustrated glass edge, clean pale turquoise water, layered paper-like pebbles, rounded underwater plants, little bubbles, one charming orange and cream moon koi with a flowing tail and curious expressive eye. The fish is carefully designed, not emoji or clipart. Strong graphic silhouettes, slight pigment grain and soft shallow diorama shadows give depth. The aquarium is a physical playful object set onto a small illustrated blue shelf line within the UI. Warm yellow space is generous. Large confident soft serif display lettering for "Fishbowl" and friendly legible rounded sans for controls. The tank name can be ink-blue handwritten-feeling rounded type but fully readable. Bottom navigation has characterful small filled illustrated glyphs, an orange selected indicator, no chunky dark dock. The editor sheet is warm white with a thin cobalt top border and rounded upper corners. Selected fish thumbnail gets a neat hand-drawn cobalt selection ring. Main button solid cobalt, white text, subtle print-like texture. Keep color punchy but limited and typography disciplined. Absolutely no candy-game coin counters, XP bars, generic cute stickers or childish clutter. It should feel like a lovingly designed little aquarium from an independent game studio, with enough craft that adults would want it as a widget.
