# Immersive liquid-glass aquarium concepts

Revised direction from user feedback: the entire screen is the aquarium front pane. Sparse, elegant aquascaping; optical glass; one discreet control. No visible bowl or app dashboard.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Visual concepts only.

## 04-clearwater

Use case: ui-mockup.
Generate one exceptionally refined iOS aquarium app design mockup for Fishbowl. The user wants liquid glass, elegance, sophistication, minimalism, and the feeling of looking directly into their own aquarium.
DELIVERABLE: one portrait image, roughly 9:16. One single complete, straight-on iPhone with an extremely thin dark titanium frame, centered, screen dominant, no perspective tilt. Device occupies 95 percent of image height; narrow unobtrusive neutral surrounding margin. Render the entire phone including softly rounded top and bottom corners. No comparison collage, no second screen, no design presentation text.
CRITICAL COMPOSITION: THE ENTIRE PHONE DISPLAY IS THE FRONT PANE OF AN AQUARIUM. Aquatic environment continues behind the iOS status bar at the very top, fills every pixel to all four screen edges, and continues behind the home indicator at the bottom. There is no smaller tank sitting within the screen. Camera is perpendicular to the glass, looking directly through the front pane and underwater. No visible bowl, orb, vessel outline, inner rounded rectangle, tank frame, exterior lip, tank pedestal, tabletop, shelf, room, border, or surrounding environment INSIDE the display. The aquarium is optically immersive, as if the smartphone glass is actual aquarium glass.
ART DIRECTION: high-end nature aquarium, immaculate clear water, masterfully sparse aquascaping, physically plausible photorealistic / offline-render quality. Quiet spatial depth. Use clear foreground, midground, background separation, natural underwater falloff and subtle caustics. A large calm volume of open water dominates the top two thirds. A single elegant fish with correct delicate anatomy and luminous translucent fins moves through that space at natural scale, approximately 25 percent of display width. Beautiful fine pale sand forms a shallow sloping foreground near the bottom. A carefully balanced asymmetrical arrangement of just three exquisite weathered stones, some precise velvety moss, and one small grouping of fine aquatic leaves anchors the lower third. No overgrown jungle, no symmetrical piles, no visual clutter, no figurines, no castle, no cartoon decorations, no fake aquarium-care statistics. All aquatic plants are rooted. The high quality and restraint of the environment should be immediately apparent.
LIQUID GLASS: optical material rather than general blur. Crystal-clear transmission, delicate refraction and subtle fluid lensing at the physical screen perimeter, a thin soft specular edge, beautifully controlled water light. NO milky glass panels, thick glossy borders, neon glow, rainbow chromatic aberration or overlaid glass cards. The only app control is ONE small circular clear liquid-glass button, 44pt visual size, near the lower right safe area above the home indicator. It contains a simple legible ellipsis. Its edge bends and magnifies the actual aquarium scene behind it; center is mostly transparent with a modest legibility treatment. No dock or bottom toolbar. This single control reveals options when needed. No on-screen brand title, tank name, navigation bar, tabs, heading, captions, stats, feeding instructions, status labels, fish badges, action buttons, or floating text. Keep only the native small 9:41 status bar and thin home indicator. More than 98 percent of the display is the aquarium. The result must feel like an expensive living aquarium viewed directly through glass, with almost no evidence of an app.

LIGHTING DIRECTION: CLEARWATER. Fresh luminous water in the most restrained pale mineral-green and silver tones. Gentle daylight filters from the top left beyond the viewport. The upper half is beautifully clear, quietly luminous and very lightly misted celadon, with believable distant depth, not an opaque gradient wall. Open water has a subtle real optical glow. Very soft natural caustic reflections graze the sand. Palette is pale celadon, ivory sand, slate-gray weathered stone, deeply considered moss-green. The main stone is an elegant dark striated vertical piece on the lower left, accompanied by two much smaller stones. The right lower area opens into a graceful sweep of pale sand and a few slender green leaves. A single extraordinary pearlescent ivory betta with a whisper of champagne and soft silvery jade in its flowing transparent fins swims slightly right of center. It has a clearly visible small dark eye, realistic proportion, luminous scales, fine branching fin rays. No orange goldfish, no oversized fish. The restrained subject is clearly distinguished from the pale water by subtle tonal shaping. Ultra-clean water, sparse visible particles. Overall light, fresh, calm, architectural. The feeling is clear fluid luxury without excess shine. Native status bar and home indicator deep slate for contrast. Surrounding margin outside the phone pale mineral gray.
